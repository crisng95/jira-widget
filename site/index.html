demoSpot a stale ticket and open it in your browser; approve what waits for you on Jira web. Then drag the panel to sit with your Apple widgets, open Claude Code, and get to work. The panel stays read-only — every action happens in Jira.

features

Built for team leads. Handy in standups.

Everything you glance at ten times a day, without opening a browser tab.

done/total

Sprint progress

Done vs. total and story points with explicit denominators — "pending release" split out from truly done, so 37/46 never lies to you.

stale > 3d

Risk alerts

Stale tickets nobody has touched, tickets that won't make it before the sprint ends, and long-lived tickets that deserve a look.

waiting-on: you

Handover queues

Waiting for test, waiting for your approval, waiting for release — filtered by role, so the queue that needs you is never empty by accident.

per-member

Team load

Progress bars for every member and a workload donut for the whole sprint — including the teammate who finished everything and vanished from other tools.

grouped ×3

Native notifications

Status changes, reassignments, tickets added or pulled mid-sprint. Bursts are grouped into one summary instead of ten pings.

team ⇄ me

One panel, two views

Switch between whole-team and only-my-tickets from the menu bar. Desktop layer keeps it under every window; floating mode keeps it on top.

transparency

Private by design. Nothing to hide.

We built the panel we'd trust with our own company's Jira credentials — here is exactly what it does, in the form an auditor would write it.

network.destinations
your sprint data flows only between your Mac and the Jira host you configure — never to us.
write.access
none — read-only by construction; the app never changes anything in Jira.
credential.storage
macOS Keychain, on your device; sent only to the Jira host you configure.
telemetry
none — no analytics, no crash reporters, no third-party SDKs. Label: "Data Not Collected".
local.data
plain files you can open and delete — config and logs, nothing hidden.
affiliation
independent product — not affiliated with or endorsed by Atlassian.
